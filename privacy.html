Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Inkrail (“we”, “us”) operates inkrailco.github.io and related digital products such as Flow Score. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Flow Score answers: Questionnaire responses are processed in your browser to compute a score. We do not require an account for the free teaser.
  • Waitlist / contact email: If you email us or use the waitlist mailto flow, we receive the address and message you send.
  • Purchases: Payments are processed by our merchant of record, Lemon Squeezy. We receive order and license details needed to fulfill digital delivery; we do not store your full card numbers.
  • License key check: If you enter a Flow Score license key, your browser sends that key directly to Lemon Squeezy’s public License API to confirm it is valid. Your unlock status, license key, and quiz answers are stored only in your browser (localStorage) so you can come back to your report; clear site data to remove them.
  • Technical data: Standard web logs may include IP address, browser type, and pages visited when hosted on GitHub Pages or similar providers.

How we use information

  • To provide and improve Flow Score and related digital products
  • To fulfill purchases, send license unlocks, and provide support
  • To respond to waitlist or contact messages
  • To comply with law and protect against abuse

What we do not do

  • We do not log into your ESP (e.g. Klaviyo) or modify your email accounts.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use Flow Score answers to contact your customers.

Third parties

Payment and tax handling for paid unlocks may be performed by Lemon Squeezy (Sold through Link, LLC). Their privacy terms apply to checkout data they process. Hosting may be provided by GitHub Pages or similar static hosts.

Data retention

Browser-local waitlist intents, quiz answers, and unlock/license data stay on your device until you clear site data. Emails you send us are retained as needed for support and operations. Purchase records follow Lemon Squeezy and our accounting needs.
