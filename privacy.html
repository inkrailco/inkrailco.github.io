Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Inkrail (“we”, “us”) operates inkrailco.github.io and related digital products such as Flow Score. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Flow Score answers: Questionnaire responses are processed in your browser to compute a score and report. We do not require an account.
  • Contact email: If you email us, we receive the address and message you send.
  • Technical data: Standard web logs may include IP address, browser type, and pages visited when hosted on GitHub Pages or similar providers.
  • Analytics: We use GoatCounter to count page views and one anonymous event (quiz completed). It sets no cookies, does not store IP addresses, and keeps only aggregate counts (such as page, referrer or campaign, browser, country, and screen size); no personal data is stored. Your quiz answers, score, and email are never sent to it.

How we use information

  • To provide and improve Flow Score and related digital products
  • To respond to contact messages
  • To comply with law and protect against abuse

What we do not do

  • We do not log into your ESP (e.g. Klaviyo) or modify your email accounts.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use Flow Score answers to contact your customers.

Third parties

Hosting may be provided by GitHub Pages or similar static hosts. Anonymous, aggregate usage statistics are processed by GoatCounter (goatcounter.com).

Data retention

Browser-local quiz answers and report state stay on your device until you clear site data. Emails you send us are retained as needed for support and operations.
